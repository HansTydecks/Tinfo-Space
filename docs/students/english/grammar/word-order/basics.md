# 1 · Subject – verb – object <Badge type="tip" text="class 5" />

## Goals
- build statements in the order **subject – verb – object**
- keep **S + V** together – also after *Yesterday*, *Today*, *On Monday* …
- never put anything between **verb and object**
- know that English **always** needs a subject (*It is raining*)

Every English statement starts with the same three wagons: **who** – **does what** – **to what / whom**.
*Im Englischen steht das Subjekt fast immer direkt vor dem Verb – egal, was am Satzanfang steht.*

## The basic order {#svo}

| S – subject | V – verb | O – object | Deutsch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tom** | **plays** | **football**. | Tom spielt Fußball. |
| **My sister** | **has** | **a cat**. | Meine Schwester hat eine Katze. |
| **We** | **watched** | **a film**. | Wir haben einen Film angesehen. |
| **I** | **can’t find** | **my keys**. | Ich finde meine Schlüssel nicht. |

The verb can have **more than one word**: *has seen, can’t find, is reading*. They are **one** wagon – the verb wagon.
*Achtung: Im Deutschen wird das Verb oft getrennt (habe … gesehen). Im Englischen bleiben die Teile zusammen: I **have seen** the film.*

### Trap 1: time at the start {#time-first}

In German, the verb stays in **2nd place** – so the subject moves behind it: *Gestern **ging ich** ins Kino.*
In English, **S + V** never change places in a statement:

| ✗ wrong (German order) | ✓ right |
|---|---|
| *Yesterday went I to the cinema.* | Yesterday, **I went** to the cinema. |
| *Today have we a test.* | Today, **we have** a test. |
| *On Mondays plays Tom football.* | On Mondays, **Tom plays** football. |
| *Then said she nothing.* | Then **she said** nothing. |

::: tip Easy check
Cover the time word with your finger. The rest must be a normal sentence: ~~Yesterday,~~ **I went to the cinema.** ✓
:::

### Trap 2: nothing between verb and object {#verb-object}

In German, you can put a lot between the verb and the object: *Ich mag **sehr gern** Pizza.* In English you can’t.

| ✗ wrong | ✓ right |
|---|---|
| *I like very much pizza.* | I like **pizza very much**. |
| *She speaks very well English.* | She speaks **English very well**. |
| *We visited last week our grandma.* | We visited **our grandma last week**. |
| *He plays often computer games.* | He **often plays** computer games. ([→ chapter 3](./adverbs)) |

## Every sentence needs a subject {#subject}

In English, the subject wagon is **never empty**. If there is no real subject, you use **it** or **there**:

| German | English |
|---|---|
| Es regnet. | **It** is raining. |
| Heute ist es kalt. | **It** is cold today. |
| Es ist schön, dich zu sehen. | **It** is nice to see you. |
| Es gibt einen Park in der Nähe. | **There is** a park near here. |
| Es gibt viele Läden. | **There are** a lot of shops. |

::: warning Imperatives
Only commands have no subject: ***Open** the window, please!* – ***Don’t** forget your homework!*
:::

## Negatives {#negatives}

The negative goes **after the helping verb**: **don’t / doesn’t / didn’t** + verb – or **not** after *be, can, have, will* …

| | ✓ | ✗ |
|---|---|---|
| no helping verb → **do** | I **don’t like** spiders. | ✗ *I like not spiders.* |
| he / she / it | She **doesn’t play** tennis. | ✗ *She plays not tennis.* |
| past | We **didn’t go** out. | ✗ *We went not out.* |
| be | Tom **isn’t** at home. | |
| can / will / have | I **can’t swim**. · She **won’t come**. · I **haven’t finished**. | |

More about *do*: [chapter 4 · questions & negatives](./questions#negatives).

## Typical mistakes {#mistakes}

::: danger Typical mistakes
✗ *Yesterday played we football.* → ✓ Yesterday, **we played** football.
✗ *I like very much chocolate.* → ✓ I like **chocolate very much**.
✗ *Is cold today.* → ✓ **It** is cold today.
✗ *Gives it a cinema here?* → ✓ **Is there** a cinema here?
✗ *I play not the guitar.* → ✓ I **don’t play** the guitar.
:::

## Check yourself {#check-yourself}

Put the words in the right order.

1. last night / we / a great film / watched
2. very much / my dog / I / love
3. raining / is / it / again
4. tomorrow / a test / have / we
5. not / my brother / likes / does / fish
6. a new pool / there / in our town / is

::: details Solutions
1. **We watched a great film last night.** (or: Last night, we watched a great film.)
2. **I love my dog very much.** – nothing between *love* and *my dog*
3. **It is raining again.** – English needs a subject
4. **We have a test tomorrow.** (or: Tomorrow, we have a test.)
5. **My brother does not like fish.** (= doesn’t like)
6. **There is a new pool in our town.**
:::
