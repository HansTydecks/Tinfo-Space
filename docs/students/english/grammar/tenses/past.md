# 2 · Simple past <Badge type="tip" text="class 5–6" />

## Goals
- build the **simple past** of regular verbs (*played*) and irregular verbs (*went*)
- use **was / were** and **did / didn’t**
- know: the simple past is for a **finished time** in the past

You use the simple past to tell **what happened** – at a time that is **over**: yesterday, last week, in 2019.
*Das simple past erzählt, was zu einem abgeschlossenen Zeitpunkt in der Vergangenheit passiert ist.*

## When do you use it? {#use}

<TenseGraphic
  name="past"
  caption="The time is over – last Saturday is finished. That’s why we use the simple past."
/>

| use | example | Deutsch |
|---|---|---|
| an action at a **finished** time | I **visited** my grandma **last weekend**. | Ich habe letztes Wochenende meine Oma besucht. |
| actions **one after the other** in a story | She **opened** the door, **looked** around and **screamed**. | Sie öffnete die Tür, … |
| habits in the past | When I was little, I **played** with Lego every day. | Als ich klein war, … |

::: tip German „habe … gemacht“ – English simple past!
In German we often say *Ich **habe** gestern Fußball **gespielt***. In English this is **not** *have played*!
If you say **when** (yesterday, last week …), you need the **simple past**: *I **played** football yesterday.*
More about this in [chapter 3](./present-perfect).
:::

## Form {#form}

| | all persons (I, you, he, she, it, we, they) |
|---|---|
| ✅ positive | She **played** tennis. – She **went** home. |
| ❌ negative | She **didn’t play** tennis. – She **didn’t go** home. |
| ❓ question | **Did** she **play** tennis? – Where **did** she **go**? |
| 💬 short answer | Yes, she **did**. / No, she **didn’t**. |

Good news: the simple past has the **same form for all persons** – no -s for he/she/it!

::: warning After did / didn’t: the infinitive
*did* already shows the past. So the verb goes back to the **infinitive**:
✗ *Did you saw it?* → ✓ **Did** you **see** it? · ✗ *I didn’t went.* → ✓ I **didn’t go**.
:::

### Regular verbs: -ed {#regular}

| rule | examples |
|---|---|
| most verbs: + **-ed** | play → play**ed**, watch → watch**ed** |
| verb ends in **-e**: + **-d** | live → live**d**, like → like**d** |
| consonant + **y** → **-ied** | study → stud**ied**, carry → carr**ied** (but: play → play**ed**) |
| short vowel + one consonant: double it | stop → sto**pp**ed, plan → pla**nn**ed |

### Irregular verbs – the top 20 {#irregular}

| infinitive | simple past | | infinitive | simple past |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| be | **was / were** | | go | **went** |
| have | **had** | | see | **saw** |
| do | **did** | | come | **came** |
| say | **said** | | get | **got** |
| make | **made** | | give | **gave** |
| take | **took** | | find | **found** |
| know | **knew** | | think | **thought** |
| eat | **ate** | | buy | **bought** |
| write | **wrote** | | leave | **left** |
| run | **ran** | | tell | **told** |

### was / were {#was-were}

| | I / he / she / it | you / we / they |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ | I **was** tired. | They **were** at home. |
| ❌ | He **wasn’t** there. | We **weren’t** late. |
| ❓ | **Was** she happy? | **Were** you at the party? |

With *was / were* you don’t need *did*: ✗ *Did you be at home?* → ✓ **Were** you at home?

## Signal words {#signal-words}

| signal words for the simple past | example |
|---|---|
| **yesterday**, the day before yesterday | I **saw** him **yesterday**. |
| **last** week / month / year / Monday | We **moved** here **last year**. |
| two days **ago**, a long time **ago** | She **left** ten minutes **ago**. |
| **in** 2019, **in** July | I **was born in** 2012. |
| **when** I was six | **When** I **was** six, I **lived** in Berlin. |

## Typical mistakes {#mistakes}

::: danger Typical mistakes
✗ *I have seen him yesterday.* → ✓ I **saw** him yesterday.
✗ *Did you went to the party?* → ✓ **Did** you **go** to the party?
✗ *She didn’t liked it.* → ✓ She **didn’t like** it.
✗ *I goed home.* → ✓ I **went** home.
✗ *They was at school.* → ✓ They **were** at school.
:::

## Check yourself {#check-yourself}

Put the verbs into the simple past.

1. Last summer we ___ (go) to Italy.
2. ___ you ___ (see) the film yesterday?
3. She ___ (not finish) her homework.
4. Tom ___ (be) ill last week, so he ___ (stay) at home.
5. I ___ (buy) a new bike two days ago.
6. Where ___ (be) you at 8 o’clock?

::: details Solutions
1. Last summer we **went** to Italy.
2. **Did** you **see** the film yesterday?
3. She **didn’t finish** her homework.
4. Tom **was** ill last week, so he **stayed** at home.
5. I **bought** a new bike two days ago.
6. Where **were** you at 8 o’clock? (no *did* with *be*!)
:::
