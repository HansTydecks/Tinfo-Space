# 1 · Simple present vs present progressive <Badge type="tip" text="class 5" />

## Goals
- build the **simple present** (*I play, she plays*) and the **present progressive** (*I am playing*)
- know **when** to use which form
- use **signal words** and **state verbs** (*like, know, want*) correctly

Both forms are **present tense**. The difference is the **aspect**: do you look at something that happens **again and again** – or at something that is going on **right now**?
*Beide Formen sind Gegenwart. Der Unterschied: Gewohnheit / allgemein gültig (simple) oder gerade jetzt im Gange (progressive).*

## The difference {#difference}

<TenseGraphic
  name="present"
  caption="Simple present: again and again, every Saturday. Present progressive: going on right now."
/>

| | simple present | present progressive |
|---|---|---|
| **use** | habits, routines – things that happen **again and again** | things that are going on **now**, at the moment |
| | facts – things that are **always true** | things that are only **for a short time** (temporary) |
| **examples** | I **play** football every Saturday. | Look! Tom **is playing** football. |
| | Water **boils** at 100 °C. | **I’m staying** with my aunt this week. |
| **Deutsch** | Ich spiele jeden Samstag Fußball. | Ich spiele gerade Fußball. |

## Form: simple present {#form-simple}

| | I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ positive | I **play** football. | She **plays** football. |
| ❌ negative | I **don’t play** football. | She **doesn’t play** football. |
| ❓ question | **Do** you **play** football? | **Does** she **play** football? |
| 💬 short answer | Yes, I **do**. / No, I **don’t**. | Yes, she **does**. / No, she **doesn’t**. |

::: tip he, she, it – don’t forget the -s!
- most verbs: + **-s** → play**s**, read**s**
- after -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: + **-es** → watch**es**, go**es**, do**es**, wash**es**
- consonant + y: **-ies** → stud**ies**, fl**ies** (but: play**s**, because there is a vowel before the y)
- **have** → **has**
- With **doesn’t** and **does**, the -s is already in *does*: ✗ *She doesn’t plays* → ✓ *She doesn’t **play***.
:::

## Form: present progressive {#form-progressive}

**am / is / are** + **verb-ing**

| | example |
|---|---|
| ✅ positive | **I’m** read**ing**. – **She’s** read**ing**. – **They’re** read**ing**. |
| ❌ negative | **I’m not** reading. – She **isn’t** reading. – They **aren’t** reading. |
| ❓ question | **Are** you reading? – **Is** she reading? – What **are** they reading? |
| 💬 short answer | Yes, I **am**. / No, **I’m not**. – Yes, she **is**. / No, she **isn’t**. |

::: tip Spelling of -ing
- most verbs: + **-ing** → read**ing**, play**ing**
- silent **-e** goes away → mak**ing**, writ**ing**, com**ing**
- short vowel + one consonant: double it → si**tt**ing, ru**nn**ing, swi**mm**ing, sto**pp**ing
- **-ie** → **-ying** → l**ying**, d**ying**
:::

## Signal words {#signal-words}

| simple present | present progressive |
|---|---|
| always, usually, often, sometimes, never | now, right now, at the moment |
| every day, every week, on Mondays | Look! Listen! |
| once a week, twice a year | today, this week (only for a short time) |

::: warning Signal words help – but think!
*Today* can go with both: *I **usually walk** to school, but today **I’m going** by bus.*
The meaning decides, not the word alone.
:::

## State verbs: no -ing {#state-verbs}

Some verbs describe a **state**, not an action. They are (almost) **never** used in the progressive:

| feelings | thinking | having | senses |
|---|---|---|---|
| like, love, hate, want, need, prefer | know, think (= believe), understand, remember, believe, mean | have (= own), belong, own | see, hear, smell, taste (when they mean a state) |

- ✓ I **like** pizza. – ✗ *I am liking pizza.*
- ✓ I **know** the answer. – ✗ *I am knowing the answer.*
- ✓ This bag **belongs** to me. – ✗ *This bag is belonging to me.*

But: *have* as an **action** can be progressive: ***We’re having** lunch.* (= eating) – ***She’s having** a shower.*

## Typical mistakes {#mistakes}

::: danger Typical mistakes
✗ *She play tennis every day.* → ✓ She **plays** tennis every day.
✗ *I am go to school every day.* → ✓ I **go** to school every day.
✗ *Look! It rains.* → ✓ Look! **It’s raining**.
✗ *Do she like cats?* → ✓ **Does** she **like** cats?
✗ *I’m wanting a new phone.* → ✓ I **want** a new phone.
:::

## Check yourself {#check-yourself}

Simple present or present progressive?

1. Listen! The baby ___ (cry).
2. My dad ___ (work) in a hospital.
3. ___ you ___ (watch) TV every evening?
4. I can’t talk now – I ___ (do) my homework.
5. Water ___ (freeze) at 0 °C.
6. Tom ___ (not like) spiders.

::: details Solutions
1. Listen! The baby **is crying**. (now – *Listen!*)
2. My dad **works** in a hospital. (always true)
3. **Do** you **watch** TV every evening? (habit – *every evening*)
4. I can’t talk now – **I’m doing** my homework. (now)
5. Water **freezes** at 0 °C. (a fact)
6. Tom **doesn’t like** spiders. (state verb *like*)
:::
