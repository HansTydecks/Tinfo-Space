# 4 · Past progressive <Badge type="tip" text="class 6–7" />

## Goals
- build the **past progressive**: *was / were* + **-ing**
- tell stories with a **background action** and a **short action** that interrupts it
- use **when** and **while** correctly

The past progressive shows that something was **going on** at a time in the past – you are **in the middle** of it.
*Das past progressive zeigt: Etwas war gerade im Gange – oft als Hintergrund für eine kurze Handlung.*

## When do you use it? {#use}

  caption="Top: a long action (past progressive) is interrupted by a short action (simple past). Bottom: two long actions at the same time."
/>

| use | example | Deutsch |
|---|---|---|
| an action that was **going on at a time** in the past | At 8 o’clock yesterday I **was having** breakfast. | Gestern um 8 Uhr frühstückte ich gerade. |
| a **background** action that is **interrupted** | I **was reading** when the phone **rang**. | Ich las gerade, als das Telefon klingelte. |
| **two** actions at the **same time** | **While** Tom **was cooking**, Anna **was doing** her homework. | Während Tom kochte, … |
| the **scene** at the start of a story | The sun **was shining** and the birds **were singing** … | Die Sonne schien … |

## Form {#form}

**was / were** + **verb-ing**

| | I / he / she / it | you / we / they |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ positive | I **was** sleeping. | They **were** sleeping. |
| ❌ negative | He **wasn’t** listening. | We **weren’t** listening. |
| ❓ question | **Was** she working? | What **were** you doing? |
| 💬 short answer | Yes, she **was**. / No, she **wasn’t**. | Yes, we **were**. / No, we **weren’t**. |

## when or while? {#when-while}

| | usually with | example |
|---|---|---|
| **when** | the **short** action (simple past) | I was reading **when** the phone **rang**. |
| **while** | the **long** action (past progressive) | **While** I **was reading**, the phone rang. |

::: tip Long action + short action
**long** action (in the background) → **past progressive**
**short** action (it suddenly happens) → **simple past**
:::

::: warning Careful with the order
- *When the phone **rang**, I **was reading**.* → I was already reading – the phone interrupted me.
- *When the phone **rang**, I **read** the message.* → First the phone rang, **then** I read the message. (one after the other)
:::

## Signal words {#signal-words}

| signal words | example |
|---|---|
| **while** | **While** we were waiting, it started to rain. |
| **when** + simple past | She was sleeping **when** I came home. |
| at 5 o’clock yesterday, at that moment | **At that moment**, everybody **was looking** at me. |
| all evening, the whole day | It **was raining** **all day**. |

## Typical mistakes {#mistakes}

::: danger Typical mistakes
✗ *I read when the phone rang.* → ✓ I **was reading** when the phone rang. (background action)
✗ *While I was cooking, the phone was ringing.* → ✓ While I was cooking, the phone **rang**. (short action)
✗ *They was playing.* → ✓ They **were** playing.
✗ *I was knowing the answer.* → ✓ I **knew** the answer. (state verb – no -ing)
:::

## Check yourself {#check-yourself}

Past progressive or simple past?

1. I ___ (have) a shower when somebody ___ (knock) at the door.
2. While Mum ___ (drive), we ___ (sing) songs.
3. What ___ you ___ (do) at 9 o’clock last night?
4. Anna ___ (fall) off her bike while she ___ (ride) home.
5. It ___ (snow) when we ___ (wake) up.
6. When the film ___ (end), we ___ (go) home.

::: details Solutions
1. I **was having** a shower when somebody **knocked** at the door.
2. While Mum **was driving**, we **were singing** songs. (two long actions)
3. What **were** you **doing** at 9 o’clock last night?
4. Anna **fell** off her bike while she **was riding** home.
5. It **was snowing** when we **woke** up.
6. When the film **ended**, we **went** home. (one after the other → simple past)
:::
